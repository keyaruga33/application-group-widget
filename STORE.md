# Application Group

**Organize your apps, folders, and documents into clean, responsive containers on KDE Plasma 6.**

Tired of having 30 scattered icons all over your wallpaper, or digging through full-screen application launchers just to find your project tools? **Application Group** gives your desktop or panel a clean, organized home for everything you use every day.

Drop it onto your **desktop** as a full workspace dashboard, or tuck it into your **panel** as a single compact icon with an item counter badge. Click the panel icon, and a full popup opens right up.

### Highlights

- **Drag & Drop Anything**: Drag apps, `.desktop` files, folders, or web links straight out of Dolphin or your browser.
- **Proper `.desktop` Execution**: Dropped desktop shortcuts actually launch as applications via KDE's native launcher (never opens in a text editor).
- **Desktop & Panel Modes**: Full interactive canvas on your wallpaper, or a sleek panel icon that opens a matching popup on click.
- **The Pin Button (📌)**: Keeps the panel popup locked open while you drag in multiple files from Dolphin, so it doesn't dismiss itself every time you click away.
- **System Apps Explorer**: Search and add installed apps in real time by name, category, or description with one click.
- **Official KDE Icon Picker**: Integrated with `KIconThemes.IconDialog` to pick any icon from your active Plasma theme.
- **Drag to Reorder**: Rearrange cards directly inside the grid or list with live visual target indicators.
- **Appearance & Styling**: Grid or list views, responsive auto columns or fixed count (2–8), custom card styles (Modern Card, Frosted Glass, Flat, Outline), background opacity, and corner radius sliders.
- **Right-Click Menus Everywhere**: Right-click cards to edit, delete, or open containing directories. Right-click empty background to pin or add items even when headers are hidden.
- **Bilingual & RTL**: Full support for English and Persian, including proper right-to-left layout mirroring.
- **Zero Idle CPU**: No background polling loops; sleeps quietly when not in use.

### Installation

#### The Easiest Way (KDE Store / Discover):
1. Right-click your panel or desktop and select **Add or Manage Widgets…**
2. Click **Get New…** → **Download New Plasma Widgets**.
3. Search for **Application Group** and hit **Install**.

#### From Terminal / Source:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/keyaruga33/application-group-widget.git
cd application-group-widget
make install
```
